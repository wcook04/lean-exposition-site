From Parisi full support to deterministic QAOA circuits

A long reasoning record

Draft, 26 October 2026. Mathematical sources: OpenAI’s full-support manuscript of 27 September 2026 and QAOA manuscript of 25 September 2026, at the pinned public revision.

AI assistance. Will Cook directed the work. AI agents based on large language models produced and revised this manuscript.

The full-support manuscript proves that an admissible integrable minimizer of the zero-temperature Parisi functional for the pure zero-field Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model has Stieltjes support equal to every overlap scale in [0,1). The proof turns a hypothetical support gap into a constant interval for the order parameter. Variational contact at the ends of that interval conflicts with a strict curvature inequality inside it. Along the auxiliary diffusion, the spatial gradient of the value function is a bounded martingale. Full support makes this martingale’s second moment equal to time; its terminal correlation with Brownian motion gives the ground-state value. The manuscript’s further argument gives a smooth Stieltjes density, strictly positive on (0,1)(0,1)(0,1), and no atom at zero. Sections 9.1–9.2 derive this stronger ordinary conclusion separately from the selected formal support statement.

This record reconstructs those arguments and follows the finite Gaussian approximation into the separate QAOA manuscript. The latter claims that finite deterministic circuits approach the SK optimum when system size tends to infinity before circuit depth increases. Its circuit construction needs additional approximation, synthesis, seed-removal and transfer arguments. The two formal statements concern full support and scalar value consequences; neither states QAOA circuit convergence. Their verification scope is described at the end. See OpenAI (2026a), §§1–2 and OpenAI (2026b), Theorem 1.1.

The support and scalar arguments assume real analysis, stochastic calculus and variational minimization. Section 8 additionally uses Hilbert-space tensor products, symmetric Fock space, unbounded operators and quantum control. Its weighted-domain and control estimates are additional analytic inputs from the QAOA manuscript; the text gives their precise uses and proof destinations. The companion short paper concentrates on full support and the strictly positive interior density, while the exposition introduces the support argument through examples. References to OpenAI (2026a) and OpenAI (2026b) distinguish the two manuscripts throughout.

Sections 1–8 give the continuous proof: full support, the terminal value, finite Gaussian correlations and the construction of one ordinary QAOA word for each accuracy. Section 9 develops the density and finite-disorder consequences. Section 10 gives an independent scalar comparison and a heat-family lower bound; Section 11 treats alternative circuit constructions and further limits. The exact formal statements and model comparison are in Section 12, followed by the source and checking record in Section 13.
