Full support and positive density in the zero-temperature SK model

A reconstruction of OpenAI’s quantitative argument · 26 October 2026

Abstract. The Stieltjes measure of every integrable minimiser of the zero-temperature Parisi functional for the pure, zero-field Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model has full support on [0,1)[0,1)[0,1) and a smooth density that is strictly positive on (0,1)(0,1)(0,1). We reconstruct OpenAI’s quantitative argument, organised around two uses of one spatial-moment inequality. On a positive constant interval, it makes the derivative of the gradient second moment strictly convex, contradicting the endpoint conditions imposed by minimality. The same curvature and the bounded gradient also exclude terminal gaps. Full support then gives moment identities and smoothness; a local constant replacement transfers the inequality to the minimiser and forces its density to be positive. H.-B. Chen previously proved full support and smooth nonnegative density. We isolate the fixed-time spatial moment and the positive margin that survives local replacement, while importing its shape comparisons and full polynomial identity. We also derive the scalar value identity and explain its classical optimisation consequence. For the quantum approximate optimization algorithm (QAOA), we identify the rooted energy of a finite Gaussian observable and the separate circuit theorem that approximates it. A further second-moment theorem gives a measured near-optimal configuration on the same random instance, with the circuit word fixed before the number of spins grows.
