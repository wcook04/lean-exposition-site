Why the Sherrington–Kirkpatrick order parameter has no gaps

Parisi’s formula gives the largest energy per spin in the Sherrington–Kirkpatrick (SK) spin glass as a minimum over nondecreasing functions, called order parameters. A gap is an interval on which an order parameter stays flat. A minimising order parameter has none: it increases across every interval in [0, 1), and its derivative is positive between 0 and 1.
